Ana Kamkhadze

Attorney at Law · U.S. Immigration & Nationality Law

Ana Kamkhadze is a licensed U.S. immigration attorney and the founder of Kamkhadze PA, a law firm exclusively practicing U.S. Immigration and Nationality Law. With nearly a decade of focused experience, Ana has built her practice on one conviction: exceptional individuals deserve exceptional legal representation.

Prior to founding Kamkhadze PA, Ana acquired substantial expertise in immigration and naturalization law, working on complex employment-based petitions for individuals of extraordinary ability across science, business, education, and the arts. Her academic background is equally distinguished — she holds a Master of Laws (LL.M.) and a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from Florida International University, as well as a law degree from Georgia.

This dual expertise in law and business gives Ana a unique advantage in immigration matters where professional achievements, corporate structures, and investment decisions intersect with visa and green card strategy. She approaches each matter with the precision of a counselor and the strategic thinking of a business advisor.

Because immigration law is federal law, Ana represents clients in immigration matters throughout all 50 U.S. states and internationally — leveraging technology to provide seamless, fully remote legal services to clients around the world.

Ana is a member of the American Immigration Lawyers Association (AILA) and the New York State Bar Association (NYSBA), and has been recognized by Best Lawyers® on its prestigious Ones to Watch list in U.S. Immigration Law for 2026 — an honor recognizing attorneys who demonstrate exceptional performance early in their careers.
