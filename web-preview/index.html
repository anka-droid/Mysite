Flagship Practice Areas

Elite Visa & Green Card Representation

Kamkhadze PA is recognized for its depth of expertise in extraordinary ability immigration — guiding the world's top talent through the most prestigious U.S. visa and green card categories.

O-1A Visa

Extraordinary Ability in Science, Business & Athletics

Reserved for individuals who have risen to the top of their field. A pathway for researchers, executives, scientists, entrepreneurs, and athletes recognized nationally or internationally for their achievements.

Explore O-1A

EB-1A Green Card

Permanent Residence for Those of Extraordinary Ability

The EB-1A grants lawful permanent residence to individuals of extraordinary ability — no employer sponsorship required. A self-petition pathway for professionals at the pinnacle of their careers.

Explore EB-1A

All Practice Areas

O-1B

Arts, Film & Entertainment

Extraordinary achievement in the arts, motion picture, or television industries.

EB-1B

Outstanding Researchers & Professors

Green card for internationally recognized academic researchers and professors.

EB-1C

Multinational Managers & Executives

Permanent residence for senior executives transferring within a multinational company.

EB-2 NIW

National Interest Waiver

Self-petition green card for professionals whose work is in the national interest of the U.S.

H-1B

Specialty Occupation

Work visa for professionals in specialty occupations requiring a degree or equivalent.

L-1A / L-1B

Intracompany Transferee

Visa for managers, executives, and specialized knowledge workers transferring within a company.

E-2

Treaty Investor

Visa for nationals of treaty countries investing substantial capital in a U.S. enterprise.

TN

Canadian & Mexican Professionals

Nonimmigrant status for Canadian and Mexican citizens under USMCA.

About the Attorney

Precision. Strategy.
A Decade of Expertise.

Ana Kamkhadze brings nearly a decade of exclusive focus on U.S. Immigration and Nationality Law. Holding both a Master of Laws and an MBA from Florida International University, she combines rigorous legal knowledge with strategic business acumen — an approach uniquely suited to the complex intersection of talent, enterprise, and immigration law.

Licensed in New York and representing clients nationwide and internationally, Ana's practice spans the full spectrum of business immigration — from extraordinary ability visas to investor pathways to family-based petitions.
