Different Worlds

The phrase “extraordinary ability” appears in both the O-1A nonimmigrant visa and the EB-1A immigrant petition. But beneath that shared language lie meaningfully different evidentiary standards, adjudicatory philosophies, and strategic implications — with consequences that matter at every stage of a talent’s immigration journey.
