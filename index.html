Nonimmigrant · Work Authorization

O-1A: Extraordinary Ability

The O-1A is the premier work visa for individuals with extraordinary ability in their field. For entrepreneurs, it offers a fast, flexible path to legally work in the U.S. — often in a matter of months.

✦
Who qualifies: Founders with demonstrable extraordinary achievement — funding raises, press coverage, speaking engagements, awards, high compensation, critical roles.
✦
Duration: Initial 3-year period, renewable indefinitely in 1-year increments.
✦
Speed: 2–3 months standard; 15 business days with premium processing.
✦
Dual intent: You can simultaneously pursue permanent residence — no need to abandon your green card plans.
✦
Entrepreneur-friendly: Works for founders, even with ownership stakes and self-employment structures, when properly structured.

Immigrant · Permanent Residence

EB-1A: Permanent Residence for the Extraordinary

The EB-1A is the gold standard for entrepreneurs seeking a U.S. green card. No employer sponsor required. No labor certification. No quota wait for most countries. Just extraordinary ability — and the right petition strategy.

✦
Self-petition: No employer sponsorship required. You control your own destiny.
✦
No PERM labor certification: Skip the 12–18 month labor certification process required by most employment-based categories.
✦
Priority date: EB-1 is the fastest employment-based green card category — current for most nationalities.
✦
National Interest Waiver alternative: We'll evaluate both EB-1A and NIW to determine the strongest path for your profile.
✦
Concurrent filing: If your priority date is current, we can file I-140 and I-485 simultaneously for faster processing.
