O-1A: Extraordinary Ability

The O-1A is the premier work visa for individuals with extraordinary ability in business, science, education, or athletics. For entrepreneurs and founders, it offers a fast, flexible path to legally work in the U.S. — often in a matter of weeks with premium processing.

✶
Who qualifies: Founders with demonstrable extraordinary achievement — funding raises, press coverage, speaking engagements, awards, high compensation, and critical leadership roles.
✶
Duration: Initial 3-year period, renewable indefinitely in 1-year increments — no cap or lottery.
✶
Speed: 15 business days with premium processing; 2–3 months standard.
✶
Dual intent: You can simultaneously pursue permanent residence — fully compatible with EB-1A green card strategy.
✶
Entrepreneur-friendly: Works for founders with ownership stakes, when properly structured with Ana's expert guidance.

Immigrant · Permanent Residence

EB-1A: Permanent Residence for the Extraordinary

The EB-1A is the gold standard for entrepreneurs seeking a U.S. green card. No employer sponsor required. No labor certification. No quota wait for most nationalities. Just extraordinary ability — and the right petition strategy from Kamkhadze PA.

✶
Self-petition: No employer sponsorship required. You control your own immigration destiny.
✶
No PERM labor certification: Skip the 12–18 month labor certification required by most employment-based categories.
✶
Fastest green card path: EB-1 is the fastest employment-based green card category — current for most nationalities.
✶
NIW evaluated alongside: Ana also evaluates EB-2 National Interest Waiver eligibility to determine your strongest path.
✶
Concurrent filing: If your priority date is current, I-140 and I-485 can be filed simultaneously for faster completion.
