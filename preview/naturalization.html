Naturalization for Lawful Permanent Residents

Naturalization is the process by which a lawful permanent resident (green card holder) becomes a citizen of the United States. It is the culmination of years of building a life in this country — and it confers the full rights of citizenship: the right to vote, to hold a U.S. passport, to petition for family members, and to remain in the United States permanently and unconditionally.

The application is filed on Form N-400, Application for Naturalization. Most applicants qualify after five years of permanent residence (three years for spouses of U.S. citizens). Kamkhadze PA reviews each client's eligibility with precision before filing — confirming continuous residence, physical presence, and good moral character — so that the application is approved on the first submission.

The firm prepares applicants thoroughly for the naturalization interview and the English and civics tests, and represents clients in matters where prior immigration history, travel records, or moral-character questions require careful legal handling before filing.

What the Law Requires of Every Applicant

Naturalization carries strict statutory requirements, and a single overlooked detail — an extended trip abroad, an unreported tax issue, an old arrest — can delay or derail an otherwise straightforward application. Kamkhadze PA examines each requirement closely before filing, so that nothing surfaces unexpectedly at the interview.

The continuous residence requirement asks that the applicant has resided in the United States as a permanent resident for the statutory period without breaking that residence through lengthy absences. The physical presence requirement asks that the applicant has actually been present in the country for at least half of that period. Both are documented carefully from travel records.

Good moral character is assessed across the statutory period and up to the day of the oath. The English and civics tests are administered at the interview — and the firm provides every applicant with the preparation needed to pass with confidence.
