Professional Biography

Ana Kamkhadze is an elite U.S. immigration attorney whose practice is devoted exclusively to U.S. Immigration and Nationality Law. Based at Hollywood Beach, Florida, she represents clients from every corner of the world — scientists, executives, artists, investors, researchers, and families — guiding them through the most complex and consequential immigration pathways available under U.S. law.

Ana's legal career is defined by a singular focus: extraordinary ability and business immigration. She has built a reputation for meticulous case strategy, compelling petition preparation, and unwavering advocacy — particularly in high-stakes visa categories where precision and depth of knowledge are paramount. Her clients include internationally recognized professionals pursuing O-1, EB-1, and EB-2 NIW pathways, as well as entrepreneurs, multinational executives, treaty investors, and families navigating the full spectrum of immigration law.

Licensed in New York, Ana practices immigration law as federal law — representing clients across all fifty U.S. states and internationally. Her firm, Kamkhadze PA, is a boutique practice built on an unapologetically high standard: every case receives the same depth of attention, the same quality of advocacy, and the same commitment to results that she would demand for herself.

Before entering private practice, Ana gained foundational legal and business training at Florida International University, where she earned both her Master of Laws (LL.M.) in U.S. Law and her Master of Business Administration (MBA) — a combination that gives her practice a distinctive edge in the complex intersection of business strategy and immigration law. She is a member of the American Immigration Lawyers Association (AILA) and the New York State Bar Association (NYSBA).
