The Consultation
Process

Every file is managed directly, intimately, and exclusively by the founding attorney from inception to completion, ensuring the highest echelon of individual attention and expertise.

01

Initial Consultation

A confidential, in-depth discussion of your background, goals, and the immigration pathways that best align with your circumstances. Conducted by Ana Kamkhadze personally.
