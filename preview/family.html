CR-1 / IR-1 / I-485

Marriage-Based Green Card

U.S. citizens and lawful permanent residents may petition for their spouses to obtain lawful permanent residence. The process involves demonstrating a bona fide marriage and navigating both USCIS and, for spouses abroad, consular processing.

We guide you through every stage — from the I-130 petition to adjustment of status or consular interview preparation — ensuring your matter is presented accurately and completely.

N-400

Naturalization & U.S. Citizenship

Naturalization is the process by which a lawful permanent resident becomes a U.S. citizen. Eligibility generally requires five years of permanent residence (three years if married to a U.S. citizen), continuous physical presence, good moral character, and passing the civics and English tests.

Kamkhadze PA provides comprehensive naturalization representation — from eligibility evaluation and application preparation to interview support and guidance through the oath ceremony.

I-130 / Family Petitions

Other Family Petitions

Beyond spousal petitions, U.S. citizens and permanent residents may sponsor other qualifying family members — including parents, children, and siblings — subject to visa availability and priority dates.
